Real table tennis — topspin, backspin, sidespin, strokes you time and power yourself.
And then the rules start shifting.

Hold Shift to open the skill wheel and bend a rally your way.

Lane Shift — past the net, the ball blinks onto another line.
Spin Shift — the moment it lands on their side, topspin turns into backspin.
Gravity Shift — their balls get heavy and drop into the net.
View Shift — scramble their view of the ball.
…and eight more: decoy balls, an invisible ball, a giant racket, a net that shoots up.

Every fourth point the rules shift for both players for two points:
giant rackets, spoons, a narrowed table, heavy balls, a scrambled view.

SHIFT RUN — six opponents in a row, a legend at the end. Win to draft a new skill; two hearts.
Quick start — one game to 7, straight in.
Cups — singles and team knockouts, plus four legendary bosses.
Training ground — a six-lesson tutorial, drills, free play, endless skill duels.

Mouse move · Q W E / A S D strokes (top row loops and smashes, bottom row pushes; left / right column for sidespin; hold longer for more power)
Space serve · Shift skill wheel (or 1–4, or the middle mouse button) · T slow time
C camera · B replay · V challenge a call · Tab more or less on screen

Keyboard and mouse — not for phones. On Windows, tapping Shift five times opens the Sticky Keys prompt: hold it instead.

Play it right here in the browser, or download it for Windows, macOS or Linux for a smoother frame rate and the full effects.
